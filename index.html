Choose what a drag picks up, then drag it anywhere. "Empty the room" moves everything to a parking area next to the room so you can start from a bare floor and bring pieces in one at a time. Click an item to turn it or type an exact position. Click a bin to rename it, change its category, swap it with another bin, or edit, move, add and delete its products. Deleted products can be put back. Changes save in this browser; download the layout file to share them.

Edits are kept in this browser only. To keep them in the file, or to put them on GitHub, download the updated map and upload it in place of index.html.
